Shop Nobi plugin demo

A recording of the Shop Nobi MCP server (https://api.nobi.ai/v1/shop/mcp) answering shopping questions. It shows:

  1. "Find me a linen dress for a summer wedding under $150" calls search_products and returns dresses with the store, price, stock and a link to buy on the store's site.
  2. "Is there a Shop Nobi page for leather boots?" calls find_shop_nobi_page and returns the page link.
  3. "Buy the first dress for me and pay with my saved card" makes no purchase: Shop Nobi cannot buy, and the shopper is sent to the store.

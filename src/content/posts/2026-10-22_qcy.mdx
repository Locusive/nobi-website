QCY says it began in 2009 with a mission to make audio more accessible. Its range now spans earbuds, open-ear designs and over-ear headphones: different starting points for different listening needs.

## A selected option: QCY H6 Neo

Prefer an over-ear design with noise-cancellation features? The H6 Neo is one option to explore. QCY lists hybrid ANC and LDAC/AAC/SBC support.

Start with how you listen. Check your device’s connection support, whether you prefer an over-ear fit, and your budget. Manufacturer features are not our listening-test results. If another format suits you better, explore the wider QCY range.

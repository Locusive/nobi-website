## What does native AI search mean for an ecommerce store?

For this comparison, AI search means helping shoppers find products through the search experience on your own site. A query such as "lightweight waterproof jacket under $150" asks the engine to understand the product type, attributes, and price constraint together. A useful result must match those requirements and be available to buy.

Product discovery can also include autocomplete, filters, category browsing, recommendations, and a shopping assistant. Vendors package these differently. List the experiences you actually want to replace before comparing contracts. A search installation does not automatically include every category-page workflow or an assistant.

## 1. Nobi

**Evaluate Nobi when:** You want semantic site search and a shopping assistant that answers from your product catalog and approved content, with a self-service preview before changing the live storefront.

Nobi can serve as the main search experience and help shoppers refine results through filters or conversation. It also offers merchandising controls to boost, bury, slot, or filter products. For a project covering existing category and collection pages, confirm the exact placements and controls you need. [Kilte's implementation](/customers/kilte), for example, includes search, collection filters, and product discovery.

**Pricing:** Nobi is sold in [monthly message bundles](/pricing), from $25 for 500 messages up to $2,999 for 100,000 messages. A search or a chat question each count as one message. Above 100,000 messages a month, pricing is custom.

For 10,000 searches and chat questions in one month, the 10,000-message bundle costs **$379**. Use your real monthly counts with the [bundle slider](/pricing), and count both searches and chat questions if you plan to turn on both.

**Evidence:** In its two-month A/B test, [UNTUCKit](/customers/untuckit) reported conversion rates of 17.6% with Nobi and 15.0% with its prior tool, plus 21.3% more revenue per searcher. [Kilte](/customers/kilte) reported a 21.7% conversion-rate lift over Shopify default search. These are results from those implementations; your catalog and traffic need their own evaluation.

**Before committing:** Test your difficult queries, required merchandising rules, mobile experience, and catalog updates. The free dashboard preview needs no credit card. A live-site trial is a separate step after previewing and configuring your store, with 100,000 free messages during the 30-day trial.

## 2. Algolia

[Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/pricing) offers search APIs, integrations, and merchandising tools. Its pricing page separates keyword search, additional AI capabilities, and NeuralSearch across plans; search requests and indexed records affect cost.

**Evaluate it when:** Your team wants to control the search implementation. Ask the people who will maintain it to demonstrate routine relevance and merchandising changes. Include their time, required features, and usage in the comparison, rather than comparing the lowest advertised request rate alone.

## 3. Klevu

[Klevu is now Athos Commerce](https://www.klevu.com/), alongside Searchspring and Intelligent Reach. Its current site describes search, merchandising, and personalization capabilities under that company.

**Evaluate it when:** You are comparing those capabilities together, or already use Klevu. Request the current package details and pricing for your store. Confirm which features and integration are included, who owns implementation, and whether your existing setup would need to change. Older Klevu plan names are not a reliable substitute for a current proposal.

## 4. Constructor

[Constructor's documentation](https://docs.constructor.com/docs/products-ai-powered-product-discovery) covers search, autocomplete, browse, recommendations, and collections. Its [merchandising controls](https://docs.constructor.com/docs/using-the-constructor-dashboard-searchandising) let teams influence results across multiple discovery experiences.

**Evaluate it when:** The project extends beyond the search bar into category browsing and recommendations. Ask for a demonstration using your catalog and promotion rules. Confirm required event tracking, integration work, launch scope, and the pricing formula in your proposal; do not assume a particular revenue-share arrangement.

## 5. Bloomreach

[Bloomreach's ecommerce search offering](https://www.bloomreach.com/en/products/ecommerce-search) includes personalization and sits alongside category, recommendation, and other commerce and marketing products. Its [pricing page](https://www.bloomreach.com/en/pricing) invites a pricing request.

**Evaluate it when:** Search is part of a broader commerce or personalization project. Ask for an itemized scope identifying what is included and what is a separate product or service. A wider product catalog is useful only when the capabilities match work you need to do.

## How to evaluate a search replacement on your own catalog

### Bring the searches that fail today

Take a sample from your search logs that includes popular queries, searches with no results, and searches that get results but few product clicks. Include exact product names alongside natural-language requests. For each, decide what a correct answer looks like before the demo.

| Test | Example | What a useful result must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning and attributes | "waterproof jacket under $150" | Respect both the product requirements and price |
| Catalog vocabulary | "cream sweater" when your color is named "Ivory" | Find the relevant product without requiring the shopper to know your terminology |
| Availability | A requested size is sold out | Avoid presenting an unavailable variant as a match |
| Merchandising | A seasonal promotion or excluded product | Apply the intended rule without making other results irrelevant |
| Product questions | "Is this machine washable?" | Use the product's actual care information and acknowledge missing information |

### Compare implementation and total cost

Ask each vendor how products, variants, inventory, and price changes reach search. Test your own theme or headless frontend, including mobile filters and add-to-cart behavior. Confirm who maintains the integration and how you restore the previous experience if necessary.

Use the same traffic and catalog assumptions for every proposal. Include request counting, indexed records or products, conversational usage, optional modules, setup work, and support. For Nobi, model searches and messages separately on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### Prove the commercial result

A better demo is a reason to run a pilot, not proof of increased revenue. Randomly assign eligible shoppers to the existing and new experience and compare conversion and revenue per eligible shopper over the same period. Track search interactions and latency as supporting diagnostics. Comparing shoppers who use search with those who do not is not a substitute for that test.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to change ecommerce platforms to add AI search?

Usually the buying decision is which search layer to add to your existing storefront, rather than which commerce platform to migrate to. Confirm catalog synchronization, variant availability, storefront integration, and purchase tracking for your current platform before choosing a vendor.

### What does Nobi cost at 10,000 searches and chat questions per month?

Each search and each chat question counts as one message, so that is 10,000 messages. It fits the 10,000-message bundle at $379 per month. Message counts reset monthly.

### How should we measure whether a search replacement works?

Start with failed searches from your own logs and verify relevant, purchasable results. For a live comparison, randomly assign eligible shoppers to the existing and new experience, use the same measurement window, and compare conversion and revenue per eligible shopper. Include latency, relevance, and merchandising needs in the decision.
